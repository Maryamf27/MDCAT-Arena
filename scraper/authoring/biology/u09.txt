M|Which band corresponds to the entire length of the thick filaments?|A band|I band|H zone|Z line|The A band is anisotropic.
M|The H zone is the region of the sarcomere containing|Only thick filaments|Only thin filaments|Overlap of thick and thin|Z lines|The H zone lies at the centre of the A band.

@ 9.5
